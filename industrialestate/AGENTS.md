# CityMETER Industrial Estate — owner preferences

Set 2026-11-10 (release 1.0.0) on the owner request “ช่วยสร้างเดโม Industrial Estate และปรับ Factory ให้สอดคล้องกัน … ทำให้สองเดโมเป็นประสบการณ์ที่ต่อเนื่องกัน” and “ถ้าไม่มีโลโก้นิคม ใช้โลโก้บริษัทหรือบริษัทแม่ได้ แต่ยังคงให้สั้นเหมือนเดิม”. The demo lives inside the Factory repository (`industrialestate/`), shares its LDS assets and citymeter-shell, and is published at https://montri-th.github.io/factory/industrialestate/ and https://citymeter.pages.dev/industrialestate/.

- Apply LDS 0.9.7 (v0.9.7-owner.1, standalone-0.9.7-r1, color-srgb-10) exactly as CityMETER Factory does: colours only from `assets/theme.css`, `js/scales.js` and `js/cues.js`; the estate fill is the 41-sample LDS `count` ramp over IEAT directory listings; an estate with no listing is EVID-05 no data (the hatch), never 0. Foundation lines (outlines, provinces) are never data colours.
- The data contract is `industrial-estate-demo/1.0.0` of the registry (`research.TH.industrial_estates`): one row per catalogue record (106); listings are IEAT directory names (snapshot 2026-11-04), never verified factories, tenants, companies or workers; name candidates (FID) are never summed across estates; verified factories are no data; no ranking of estates (not approved). Province = the source label; nothing is placed by point-in-polygon.
- Geometry: only the 46 OpenStreetMap outlines the master selected (`DEMO_BOUNDARIES`, ODbL 1.0, attribution in the map and the provenance). The other 60 records say why they have no outline; never guess a point, a circle or an area.
- The around-estate set (Factory registrations with a published point ≤ 3 km from the outline, 0 inside it) is context, never membership: headed “โรงงานรอบนิคม”, never “ในนิคม”. CityMETER Factory shows the same set (`../data/estate-context.json`); keep both built by `scripts/build-data.py` in one run.
- Logos: a file only when its SHA-256 equals `LOGO_LIBRARY.logo_sha256`; say what the mark is in two or three words (โลโก้นิคม · บริษัทแม่ · ผู้พัฒนา) and keep labels short (กนอ., no “Public Company Limited”). Never copy logo URLs.
- Privacy: a directory name written as a natural person's reads “ผู้ประกอบการบุคคลธรรมดา”, is never sent to Google and never matched to a registration. Never commit the research sheet, its exports, private Drive/Sheet URLs, credentials, Landometer prices, contracts or pipeline.
- Every panel view ends with one provenance line: dataset · source · date · boundary, then “วิธีนับ” (`DATA_METHOD.md`).
- Thai and English on every page (`?lang=en`, citymeter-shell `js/shell/i18n.js`); names without English in the source stay Thai. Review at 390 px and desktop, light and dark, before claiming done; local renders are not proof of a live release.
- `js/shell/` and `navigation.css` are citymeter-shell bytes: change them in every repository at once (`js/shell/manifest.json`). Releases bump one cache tag `?v=YYYYMMDD-rN` on every entry and the import map in `index.html`.

## Search — names found the way people type them (owner 2026-11-12)

- Since release 1.2.0 of this page (with Factory 0.9.0) (citymeter-shell 0.7.0; owner 2026-11-12 12:05, VERBATIM: “ศึกษาแนวคิดเรื่อง Fuzzy search แล้ว implement กับ demo web ให้ได้ user benefit สูงสุดตาม user intent ในขณะนั้น และตาม product statement”; 13:15 “รวม university ไว้ด้วยกันเลย”) the search box lists names instead of filtering while it is typed in. `js/shell/fuzzy.js` is the matcher — the method of the CityMETER area-search brief (02-CityMETER__fuzzy-search-tech-brief v1, 19 Oct 2026, revised 20 Oct), moved from PostgreSQL to the browser because a demo is a static site: a name as typed, without its tone marks, by letters that sound alike, with its lead word (จ. อ. เขต) or the place it is in, typed on the other keyboard layout, or one or two keys off; the last three are guesses, made only when the sure steps find little. `js/shell/search.js` is the list under the box (a WAI-ARIA combobox in the flow of the panel) and `js/find.js` gives it this demo's names: provinces, industrial estates (also by their name without นิคมอุตสาหกรรม, and in English), estate types and developers.
- Intent decides the action: a province opens its estates, an estate its page, a type its filter, a developer its name as the words of the text filter (it has no filter of its own). Enter with nothing chosen applies the words as the text filter, as the box always did; when the words keep no row, Enter marks the first name found as typed and Enter again opens it — a row is never opened unmarked. The map and the panel do not change while the reader types.
- What is chosen is shown. A filter or words chosen from the list apply to the whole page: the place being looked at is left (the province stays — a province without the brand is a reading of that province) and the other filters that would leave the choice no row are lifted first, the fewest that do (`makeWay` → `filtersToLift`, js/shell/search.js). An item chosen always opens: the filters it does not pass are lifted (`makeWayFor` → `filtersAgainst`). The `filters` option of `installSearch` (js/app.js) names the filters that may be lifted — never a counting unit or a measure. The number on the list's last row is the rows the words keep with the filters that are on — or, when those leave them no row, with the ones that stay once the fewest are lifted, as choosing the row does (`rowsAfterWay`).
- A guess is never chosen for the reader (the brief's decision of 10 Oct 2026: “เลือกแถวให้อัตโนมัติเฉพาะเมื่อมีพื้นที่เดียวที่ชื่อตรง”). Only an entry that alone is written with the very name typed, or that name with its place, is marked ahead — never an estate that shares its name with another, and never an entry that only goes by the name once its head word is left out (“ภูเก็ต” is the province; เทศบาลนครภูเก็ต is found by it and is a row below).
- A row says what an entry is and where, in words: no number and no caveat. How sure a match is stands in words too — the tag “สะกดต่างจากที่พิมพ์”, the heading “ชื่อใกล้เคียง”.
- Nothing typed is stored or sent: no query log and no feedback button (the brief keeps both for after its PDPA review).
- Other names of a place are the four of `PROVINCE_AKA` (js/shell/fuzzy.js) and a district's name without เมือง. Who keeps a fuller alias list is an open decision of the brief: do not grow the list by hand. A name the data carries in one language is found in that language only.
- A new kind of entry needs its line in `searchNote` (js/find.js), its action in `openFound` (js/app.js) and, on an English page, the words of its line in js/lang-en.js (the `.find-where` context); `tests/find.test.js` checks them. The demo's own `#search` input stays the field of the applied text filter (hidden, read by the code as before); if js/shell/search.js does not load, the box filters as it always did.
- On a phone, while a name is typed the panel stands tall and the box comes to its top, so the list has room above the keyboard (`html.find-typing` in navigation.css, set by js/shell/search.js while the box has the focus).
- On a phone the search and the filters stand with the overview (`.panel:not([data-mode="overview"]) .filters` in styles.css): `renderPanel` in js/app.js must set `#panel[data-mode]`. Release 1.1.0 never set it, so on a phone this page had no search box and no filter in any view (seen on citymeter.pages.dev in a 393 px frame, 2026-11-12); 1.2.0 sets it.
- Before a release, type in the box at 393 px and on a desktop, in Thai and in English: a name as written, one without its tone marks, one with a wrong key, one on the other keyboard layout, a text that is no name — and, with a filter on, a name of another kind (the filter gives way). An estate is a level of this page, so one that alone is written with the name is marked ahead. This page keeps its English in `T(th, en)` and js/lang-en.js; `tests/find-estate.test.js` (in the repository's tests/) checks the list.
